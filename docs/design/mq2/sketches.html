Metric quality sketches

Eight static screens for metric quality, drawn with the demo workspace's own metrics and goals. Nothing here is built. Each sketch has notes beside it saying what is happening and which of your decisions it shows.

Revised after the review (15 Nov): Draft metrics warn instead of blocking; baselines are no longer needed for Defined and never lapse Verified; Verified means someone other than the owner or last editor reproduced the figure; the type is two fields (what is measured, how it is aggregated) plus Other. "Level" is now stage, because metrics already have a level (component, driver, control, breakdown).

Revised again after the twelve journeys (15 Nov): a verifier can record "Couldn't reproduce" (sketches 5 and 10); baselines from an old source are flagged for retaking (sketch 6); a goal's check-in rhythm follows its metric's refresh (sketches 7 and 10); a leaving owner's metrics pass to their lead (sketch 11). New: quarterly baselines from readings (9), the "To sort out" list (11) and the duplicate check when adding a metric (12).

Everything here can be done through MCP (sketch 13): the same rules answer an AI tool as answer the screens, so a whole register can be loaded, defined, baselined and verified from Claude without opening Faro.

Calculation, two fields

What is measured (count, sum, value, duration, ratio) and how it is aggregated (total, mean, median, pXX, latest). Lead time becomes "duration, median"; eNPS, availability or an index use Other with a formula in words. Ratio asks for a numerator and a denominator, each with its own counts, filters and exclusions.

Window

Required and structured: point in time, rolling N days, per period (week, month, quarter) or cumulative since a date. No free text.

Defined needs the full definition

Ten things: meaning, type and parts, inclusions and exclusions, unit, direction, window, source, source owner, refresh, metric owner. A baseline is no longer one of them. The box counts what's left and says why each matters.

No link, not Defined

The source link is blank, so this stays Draft even with everything else filled in.

Saving is always allowed. The metric becomes Defined by itself once the list is empty; nobody presses a button for it.

Who

Anyone but the metric's owner and whoever last changed its calculation, window or source: an admin, the source owner's people, or a named verifier. Here Amara verifies a metric Daniel owns.

One check, one figure

"I opened the link" plus the figure they reproduced, both required. The figure is the proof; the three opinion ticks are gone.

When it doesn't match

"Couldn't reproduce" saves the figure and the note without verifying. The metric stays Defined, the owner is told, and the attempt shows on the metric's page (sketch 10).

What it is compared with

The stored baseline for the period, if there is one; otherwise the latest reading from the source; otherwise nothing, and the figure is simply recorded.

A difference is shown, not judged

Faro shows the gap from the stored figure in the metric's own unit, with no fixed tolerance; the note says why it differs.

Stored

The figure, the note, who and when, and a re-verify date 12 months on. These become the record on the metric's page (sketch 2).

SettledAn admin may not verify a metric they own, or one they last changed.

Lapsing

Verified drops to Defined when the calculation parts, window, source link or direction change, or after 12 months. Baselines never lapse it. Rewording a filter asks the last verifier to confirm instead.

The reason shown

The page names the change, who made it, when, and the reason they gave, beside the old verification. The owner is told, and the metric joins "To verify". "Verify again" is the one action.

Old baselines flagged

Baselines taken from the old source are marked and the owner is asked to retake them, so a step in the chart is read as the source, not the work. The chart marks the week of the change.

The goal keeps working. It shows the metric as Defined; only Draft gets a warning on a goal.

The notice is a whole-surface box with a full outline, never a side stripe.

Warn, don't block

Draft metrics can be picked, listed after Defined and Verified, each saying what's missing. Setting goals at the start of a cycle is never held up by the library. Gap metrics are never blocked either.

Refresh in the picker

Every option shows its window and refresh beside the unit. Picking a monthly metric says the goal's check-ins become monthly.

Quick add

A new metric from the picker asks only name, unit and direction, so nobody avoids measuring a goal to dodge a form.

The warning

A goal on a Draft metric keeps its status and shows the warning mark and a sentence, and goes on a "To sort out" list for the metric's owner. The warning is not a status shape.

Wherever the goal names its metric, the stage and refresh come with it.

Same rules, one place

Stages, lapsing, who may verify and the duplicate check live once in the metrics module's rules. The screens, the import and the MCP server all call them, so an AI tool can't do what the screen wouldn't allow.

Asks, doesn't guess

Anything the screens confirm (a lapse, a similar metric, an old-source baseline) comes back as checks with nothing saved, so the tool asks the person.

Answers in words

Every result says what happened in a sentence a person can read back, and every refusal says why and who can do it instead.

Module off

With Metrics switched off, every one of these tools refuses with the module-off sentence.

Two new tools (save_baselines, verify_metric); the rest extend tools that exist today.
