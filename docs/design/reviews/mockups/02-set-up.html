Set up the review

Wireframe 2. Setting a review up, one screen, 620 px. Goals are added in one of four ways, each found by typing (the chooser is shown open on the first way): a goal and everything under it, a team’s goals, a level, or picked one by one. Who takes part is find-and-pick, with the owners of the chosen goals offered. “When” says the first dates. The last block reads it all back before anything is saved, in counts, and says when goals overlap another review. Today there is no team, the trees are a list of every top goal, the levels are capsule buttons, and nothing is read back or dated (V8, V9).
