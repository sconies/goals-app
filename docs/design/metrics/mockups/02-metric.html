Pipeline queue wait (p90)

min, whole numbers · lower is better · owned by Rhea Kapoor

See its 2 goals

What it means and how it’s calculated

How long a pipeline job waits for a runner, from the moment it is queued to the moment it starts, at the 90th percentile over the week. It counts every job on main and on merge requests. Time spent waiting for another job to finish first is left out.
