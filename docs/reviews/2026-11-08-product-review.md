# Product strategy review, 8 November 2026

The same outside reviewer's view of what Faro needs to become a habit. Judged by the planning session against what Faro is for (a model to copy plus a demo, not a product to sell), with Lane's choices of 8 Nov.

## Adopted (backlog)
- **Reward honesty and early warnings, never shame.** A design rule the rebuild must keep: reporting "off track" early is never punished; no public ranking of red goals. (R5, with 8r's requirements.)
- **The habit measure:** % of goals checked in before the review without a nudge. On the VP's Home and in the spec. (L)
- **Review carry-forward:** decisions and actions from one review open the next ("last week you said you'd do X: did it happen?"), if Faro doesn't already. (L)
- **A target-hit celebration:** a restrained moment, plus the existing share image, when a goal crosses its target. (L)
- **Numbers that fill themselves in:** a number-source interface with one demo source; real connectors (code hosting, CI, incidents, support tools) are the internal team's. (8c, with 0b's metric source)
- **Platform items as requirements:** digests in Slack, Teams or email; check-in from a notification; mobile push; view-only stakeholders and finer roles; Cmd-K search. (R5)
- **First win at the end of set-up; default saved views for a new workspace.** (S)

## Ideas, not yet placed
- Team streaks with a grace week (never loss-framed).
- Positive-only spotlights: best comeback, best early warning, most improved.
- Portfolio view for a VP: what moved across the division this week, forecast movers, quietly stale goals, teams overloaded with red.
- A quarter story, auto-written, shareable (builds on the existing wrap-up and share image).

## Not doing
- Removing count-up animation outright: Lane's requirement is that numbers change visibly when a check-in posts. The fault is passing through wrong values on page load; D item 16 fixes that.
- Merging List and Table: no evidence; they serve different jobs.
- A per-person check-in heatmap (weekly cadence gives ~52 squares, invites comparing people, rewards filling squares over honest numbers); in its place a per-team and per-goal **check-in rhythm strip** joins L (Lane, 8 Nov).
- Density mode, badges, kudos and a Friday Wins wall: they fight "calm, low density, one main action" and risk dishonest reporting.
- Motion lab behind a Labs switch: harmless either way.
