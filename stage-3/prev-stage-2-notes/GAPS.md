## Things in the kit that were wrong or contradictory

- scorecard.md: "The conformance data never shows one [a stopped goal's hit cell]". It does show the case, as an absent row in weeks before the stop (#1).
- projection.md: "every earlier check-in … even two in one week" contradicts what the code does for the latest week (#3).
- projection.md: "a range with its limit alone" contradicts its own between-range example (#9).
- unmeasured.md: "Its calculation is none" without scope, while check-in judging and the scorecard ignore it (#2).
